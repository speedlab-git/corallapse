Summary

The 2019 and 2022 mosaics of plot T3 differ strongly in colour (ΔE 31.3; mean hue 99° vs 71°), consistent with different water and light conditions. Standard matching found only 4–7 consistent correspondences, so colony-level change cannot be assessed from the published mosaics; joint alignment of the raw images is required. Benthic labels are available from the Pulley Ridge training set.
